Privacy notice

How booking information is used.

Information collected

When you book a lesson, the booking system collects your full name and mobile number, together with the lesson you selected.

Why it is used

This information is used to manage your padel lesson, identify who is attending and allow José to contact you about your booking when necessary.

What other players can see

Only your first name is displayed publicly beside the lesson you have booked. Your full name and mobile number are available only within the private coach dashboard.

Payments

No card or payment information is collected through this website. Payment is made directly to José in person.

Booking records

Booking information is stored securely for the purpose of administering coaching sessions. If you want information about a booking corrected or removed, contact José directly.

Third-party services

The website uses hosting and database infrastructure to operate the booking service. The Add to Calendar feature opens Google Calendar only when you choose to use it.
