MEET YOUR COACH

Spanish padel experience, coached your way.

José Arrayás is a Spanish padel coach and competitive player based in Dublin. With over five years of experience in padel and coaching, he combines his experience as a player with a dynamic and practical approach to teaching.

A certified coach, José works with players of all levels, helping them develop their technical, tactical and game awareness while keeping every session enjoyable and motivating. José is currently ranked #9 in Ireland in the Open Men rankings by Padel Federation Ireland.
